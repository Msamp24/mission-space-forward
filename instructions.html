🚀 Mission SPACE

Activity Instructions

Mission SPACE Forward

A card-based team activity for reflecting on your current work practices. Gain insights to how your team is working now, what you would like to experience more of, and what could help you move forward.

1 · Set up 2 · How to play 3 · Review your board 4 · Reflect & moving forward
1

Step one

Set up

Prepare the cards

  • Separate the Statement Cards and Reflection Cards.
  • Put the Reflection Cards to one side for now. You will use them at the end.
  • Shuffle the Statement Cards.
  • Deal 7 Statement Cards to each player.
  • Place the remaining Statement Cards face down.
  • Put the Mission SPACE Forward board where everyone can see and access it.
2

Step two

How to play

A

Choose a SPACE dimension

At the start of each round, agree which SPACE dimension you want to play:

😊 Satisfaction & Well-being 🚀 Performance ✅ Activity 💬 Communication & Collaboration ⚡ Efficiency & Flow
B

Pick a card

Select a Statement Card from your hand that matches the SPACE dimension you have chosen.

Play this card face down.

C

Reveal

Once everyone has played a card, choose one player to shuffle the played cards.

That player reads each statement aloud.

D

Discuss and place it on the board

Discuss each statement as a team, thinking about it in relation to your own work and experiences. Agree together where it belongs on the board, considering both questions:

How much does this happen?

How much does this currently happen in our team?

How much should this happen?

How much would we like to experience this in our team?

Use the 1–5 scales to agree the position, then place the card where the two ratings meet.

E

Repeat

Continue the activity, making sure you have played each SPACE dimension at least once.

Example

What a round might look like...

😊 Satisfaction
A

Agree a dimension

As a team, you agree to focus on Satisfaction & Well-being for this round.

B

Check your hand

Look for a Statement Card that matches Satisfaction, select one and place it face down.

No match? Don't worry, you can still join the discussion.

🗣️
C

Read them aloud

One team member picks up all the face-down cards, shuffles them and reads each one aloud.

D

Discuss and place

Talk through each statement and place it on the board together.

⏱️ Keep an eye on the time!

S ✓ P A C E
E

Play the next round

When all the cards are placed and you are happy with them, choose another dimension to play.

Keep an eye on the time. It is easy to lose track of time when talking about the cards, so set yourselves a time limit. You may want to spend around 5 minutes per round, depending on how many cards are played, and roughly no more than a minute per card.

Step four

Reflect & move forward

Now take the 4 Reflection Cards you put aside at the start and complete them in order: 1 → 4. Each card guides your team through the final reflection.

Take time to discuss each prompt before moving to the next one. Use your board and the discussion you have already had to help answer the questions.

1

Where do we want to be?

If this was working better, what would we notice?

💡 Look at your More of this cards

2

Has it happened before?

When has this worked better before? What was different?

3

What already helps?

What strengths or resources do we already have that could help us?

💡 Look at your Celebrate cards

4

What next?

What is one small thing we could do to move closer to where we want to be?
