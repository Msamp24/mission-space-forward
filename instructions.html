Activity Instructions · Quick Guide

Mission SPACE Forward

A card-based team activity for talking about how you work today, what you want more of, and what could help you move forward.

🎯 Your goal

Build a shared picture of how your team is working now, what you would like to experience more of, and what could help you move forward.

1 · Set up ⚙️ 2 · How to play 🃏 3 · Review your board 👀 4 · Reflect & move forward 💭
1

Step one

Set up ⚙️

Choose an Activity Lead

Pick one person to keep the activity moving and watch the time.

They do not make the decisions. Everyone contributes equally.

Prepare the cards

  • Separate the Statement Cards and Reflection Cards.
  • Put the Reflection Cards to one side for now. You will use them at the end.
  • Shuffle the Statement Cards.
  • Deal 7 Statement Cards to each player.
  • Place the remaining Statement Cards face down.
  • Put the Mission SPACE Forward board where everyone can see and reach it.
🔒Keep your hand of cards private until you play one.
2

Step two

How to play 🃏

A

Choose a SPACE dimension

At the start of each round, agree which SPACE dimension you want to play:

😊 Satisfaction & Well-being 🚀 Performance ✅ Activity 💬 Communication & Collaboration ⚡ Efficiency & Flow

Play each SPACE dimension at least once during the activity.

B

Pick a card

Select a Statement Card from your hand that matches the SPACE dimension you have chosen.

Play this card face down.

C

Reveal

Once everyone has played a card, choose one player to shuffle the played cards.

That player reads each statement aloud.

D

Discuss and place it on the board 📍

Discuss each statement as a team, thinking about it in terms of your own work and experiences. Then agree together where it belongs on the board, considering both questions:

How much does this happen?

How much does this currently happen in our team?

How much should this happen?

How much would we like to experience this in our team?

Use the 1–5 scales to agree the position, then place the card where the two ratings meet.

The placement should represent the team's agreed view, not just the view of the person who played the card.

E

Repeat 🔁

Continue the activity, making sure you have played each SPACE dimension at least once.

3

Step three

Review your board 👀

When you finish playing, look at the board as a whole. Notice where your cards have been placed.

How much does this happen? ↑

↪️ Redirect

Things that may be happening but do not closely reflect where you want to be.

🎉 Celebrate

Things that are happening and that you want to continue.

⏸️ Park

Things that are less important to focus on right now.

🌱 More of this

Things you would like to experience more often.

How much should this happen? →

Ask yourselves

  • What patterns do you notice?
  • What stands out?
  • Where are the strengths and opportunities?
4

Step four

Reflect & move forward 💭

Now take the 4 Reflection Cards you put aside at the start and complete them in order: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4. Each card guides your team through the final reflection.

Take time to discuss each prompt before moving to the next one. Use your board and the discussion you have already had to help answer the questions.

1

Where do we want to be?

If this was working better, what would we notice?

2

Has it happened before?

When has this worked better before? What was different?

3

What already helps?

What strengths or resources do we already have that could help us?

💡 Look at your 🎉 Celebrate cards

4

What next?

What is one small thing we could do to move closer to where we want to be?

💡 Look at your 🌱 More of this cards

✅Finish by agreeing on the small steps or actions your team wants to take forward.

Throughout the activity

Remember 🤝

There are no right or wrong placements.

👂 Listen to each other 🙌 Respect different perspectives 🤝 Agree placements as a team 🧭 Focus on what can help you move forward
